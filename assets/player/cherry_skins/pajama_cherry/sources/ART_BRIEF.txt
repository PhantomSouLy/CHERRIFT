CHERRIFT Pajama Cherry — built-in image generation source brief

References: accepted Base Cherry chibi proportions and pastel peach-pink palette;
existing Pajama Cherry front/profile/rear outfit sprites from PhantomSouLy/CHERRIFT.
Outfit: oversized pastel-pink sweatshirt with white bunny-face and tiny cherry
motif, scalloped pink sleep shorts, pink-white striped socks with pompoms,
plush bunny slippers. Preserve long loose hair, brown eyes with heart highlights,
bunny ears, loop ahoge, pink front strand visible only in front-facing views.

Five authored views: front/down, front three-quarter/down_right, right profile,
rear three-quarter/up_right, straight rear/up. Opposite directions are mirrored.
Each source is a 6 columns x 6 rows transparent full-body atlas:
Idle, sleepy Idle2, short alternating Walk, ranged Attack, Walk Attack, Shield Cast.
Every row contains six consecutive whole-body poses. No projectile or shield
painted on sprite; the existing Common/Sport VFX supplies the shared Shield.

Constraints: matching head scale, fixed ear height and hip alignment, generous
gutters, no clipped ears/hair/hands/slippers; small normal steps with complete
legs, no high kicks, wide stance, merged slippers, extra limbs, or bent toe-only
motion. Soft unchanged pastel colors, no background, grid, text, glow or shadow.
Rear views hide eyes/face/front graphic and show plain fur ear backs.

Export keeps each complete original figure and its alpha, removes only isolated
background residue, registers ears, exports ordinary RGBA PNGs and motion maps.
The original image-generation sources are included unchanged.
