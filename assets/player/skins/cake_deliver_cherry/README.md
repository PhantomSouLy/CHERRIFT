# Cake Deliver Cherry — Common Hybrid

Cake Deliver Cherry uses the canonical Cherry character template. Cherry's
face, warm brown eyes, peach-pink hair color and volume, bunny-ear shape, loop
ahoge, compact body proportions, scale, and four-direction silhouette remain
fixed. Only the cake-shop delivery uniform and Hybrid skill presentation
change.

## Gameplay

- Rarity: Common
- Type: Hybrid
- Passive: +1% ATK Bonus and +1% HP Bonus
- Attack: standard Base Cherry ranged attack with one pink magical orb
- Skill: Pink Burst
  - damages nearby enemies
  - knocks enemies outward
  - grants +5% Movement Speed for 1 second

## Sprite layout

- Cell size: 192×192 px
- Pivot: x=96, y=184
- Idle: 4 frames per direction
- Walk, Attack, Skill: 6 frames per direction
- Directions: Down, Up, Left, Right
- Format: RGBA PNG with transparent background
- Left sheets are exact per-frame mirrors of Right sheets

## Canonical rear-view rules

- The loop-shaped ahoge/antenna remains visible.
- The pink front hair strand is hidden.
- Bunny ears show only their plain fur-covered backs.
- No inner pink ear panels or white inner-ear tufts are visible.
- Cherry's face is never visible from the Up/rear direction.

## Outfit

- White and pastel-pink cake-shop delivery uniform
- Off-shoulder blouse with pink piping and layered ruffled sleeves
- Rose-pink ribbon choker
- Fitted white apron with pale-pink trim and bunny-emblem pocket
- Tiny cherry charm and large rose-pink back waist bow
- Dusty rose-pink fitted trousers
- White-and-pink closed shoes
- No cake, tray, drink, box, food, utensil, or other prop is baked into sprites
