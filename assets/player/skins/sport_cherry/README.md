# Sport Cherry — Common Defensive

This pack rebuilds Sport Cherry from the canonical Cherry character template.
Only the outfit and the Defensive skill presentation change; Cherry's face,
hair, colors, bunny ears, loop ahoge, body proportions, scale, and directional
silhouette follow the shared base template.

## Gameplay

- Rarity: Common
- Type: Defensive
- Passive: +2% HP Bonus
- Attack: standard Base Cherry ranged attack with a pink sport-themed cast glow
- Skill: Shield
  - duration: 2 seconds
  - blocks incoming attacks
  - restores 10% HP

## Sprite layout

- Cell size: 192×192 px
- Pivot: x=96, y=184
- Idle: 4 frames per direction
- Walk, Attack, Skill: 6 frames per direction
- Directions: Down, Up, Left, Right
- Format: RGBA PNG with transparent background
- Left sheets are exact per-frame mirrors of Right sheets

## Canonical rear-view rules

- The loop-shaped ahoge/antenna remains visible.
- The pink front hair strand is hidden.
- Bunny ears show only their plain fur-covered backs.
- No inner pink ear panels or white inner-ear tufts are visible.
- Cherry's face is never visible from the Up/rear direction.

## Outfit

- White athletic crop top with pastel-pink panels and piping
- Tiny cherry emblem
- Pastel-pink running shorts with white piping and drawstring
- Pink wristbands
- White socks with pink stripes
- Pink-and-white running shoes
