# Kimono Cherry — Common Support

Kimono Cherry uses the canonical Cherry character template. Cherry's face,
warm brown eyes, peach-pink hair color and volume, bunny-ear shape, loop ahoge,
compact body proportions, scale, and four-direction silhouette remain fixed.
Only the outfit and the Support skill presentation change.

## Gameplay

- Rarity: Common
- Type: Support
- Passive:
  - +1% Movement Speed
  - +1% HP Recovery
- Attack: standard Base Cherry ranged attack with a soft pink blossom orb
- Skill: Healing
  - restores 40% HP
  - grants +5% Movement Speed for 2 seconds

## Sprite layout

- Cell size: 192×192 px
- Pivot: x=96, y=184
- Idle: 4 frames per direction
- Walk, Attack, Skill: 6 frames per direction
- Directions: Down, Up, Left, Right
- Format: RGBA PNG with transparent background
- Left sheets are exact per-frame mirrors of Right sheets

## Canonical rear-view rules

- The loop-shaped ahoge/antenna remains visible.
- The pink front hair strand is hidden.
- Bunny ears show only their plain fur-covered backs.
- No inner pink ear panels or white inner-ear tufts are visible.
- Cherry's face is never visible from the Up/rear direction.

## Outfit

- Pale blush-pink and white floral kimono
- Sakura and tiny cherry motifs
- Deeper rose-pink obi with bow and tassel
- Wide kimono sleeves
- White tabi socks
- Pink-strapped wooden zori sandals
