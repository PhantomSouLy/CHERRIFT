# Archer Cherry

Runtime-ready 192 × 192 RGBA sprite strips for CHERRIFT.

- Canonical pivot: x=96, ground y=184
- Idle: 4 frames per direction
- Walk, ranged attack and Four Arrow Shot: 6 frames per direction
- Passive: +10% critical chance
- The moving and attack states lift the weapon clear of the ground.
- `archer_cherry_validation.json` records the per-frame alpha bounds.
